# NeerRakshak
ESP32-Based Smart Water Leakage Detection, Alert and Automatic Shut-Off System
NeerRakshak 

NeerRakshak means Water Protector — a smart water leakage detection and automatic shut-off system.

Project Overview:

NeerRakshak is an ESP32-based hardware project designed to detect water leakage, alert users, and automatically stop the water supply to reduce water wastage.

 Objectives:

- Detect water leakage using a sensor.
- Alert users when leakage is detected.
- Automatically shut off the water supply.
- Reduce water wastage and prevent water damage.

Components Used:

- ESP32 development board
- Water leakage detection sensor
- Water shut-off mechanism
- Alert device
- Connecting wires and supporting components

The final component list will be updated to match the hardware used in our prototype.

 Working Principle:

1. The sensor monitors for water leakage.
2. The ESP32 processes the sensor readings.
3. When leakage is detected, the system activates an alert.
4. The shut-off mechanism stops the water supply.

Project Goal:

To develop an affordable, practical, and reliable smart water protection system.

 Project Status:

Hardware prototype development and testing.
